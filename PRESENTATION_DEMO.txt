FINCLOUD - PRESENTATION DEMO

1. Open login.html.
2. Sign up with your name, email and password.
3. After login, your name appears on the dashboard.
4. Click Add Bank Account and enter a DEMO bank name, account number and opening balance.
5. The dashboard shows the bank balance and Total Balance.
6. Add Income and Add Expense. The dashboard updates automatically:
   Total Balance = Bank Opening Balance + Income - Expenses
7. Open Transactions to see and delete transactions.
8. Click Logout to return to login.html.

IMPORTANT: The bank feature is a presentation sandbox. It does NOT connect to a real bank. Never enter a real bank password, OTP, UPI PIN or banking credentials.
